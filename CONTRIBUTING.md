# Contributing to AntCapture

We love community contributions! To ensure we can maintain our dual-license model, we require all contributors to sign a Contributor License Agreement (CLA).

## Why a CLA?
Because AntCapture is dual-licensed (AGPL v3 + Commercial), we need to ensure that the core project has the legal right to distribute your contributions under both our open-source and commercial terms. You still own your code, but you grant us the right to use it.

## How to Sign
When you open a Pull Request (PR) on GitHub, our CLA bot will automatically add a comment with a link to sign the agreement. It takes 10 seconds and you only need to do it once.

## Contribution Guidelines
1. Fork the repository and create your branch from `main`.
2. Ensure your code matches the existing style.
3. Write clear commit messages.
4. Open a PR and sign the CLA when prompted.

Thank you for helping build AntCapture!
