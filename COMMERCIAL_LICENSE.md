## When Do You Need a Commercial License?

You **DO NOT** need a commercial license if:
- You are an individual using AntCapture for personal use.
- Your organization uses AntCapture strictly internally, has fewer than 5 users, and does not provide it as a service to third parties.
- You are building an open-source project that is also licensed under AGPL-3.0.

You **DO** need a commercial license if:
- Your organization has 5 or more users using AntCapture.
- You embed AntCapture within a proprietary (closed-source) product that you sell or distribute.
- You host AntCapture as a service for your customers (SaaS).
- You require enterprise features such as SSO (SAML), Audit Logs, or Custom White-labeling.
- You require a standard commercial software agreement and SLA.
