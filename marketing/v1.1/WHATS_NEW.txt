AntCapture v1.1 — What's New
==============================

BUG FIXES & IMPROVEMENTS

📊 Google Drive Quota Bar (Command Center)
   The Google Drive storage progress bar was not showing in the dashboard for
   connected accounts due to OAuth tokens not being forwarded to the analytics
   service. This is now fixed — the Drive quota (used / total) appears correctly
   alongside your AntCapture Cloud and Whiteboard usage bars.

🧭 Stable Sidebar on Page Reload
   The sidebar navigation items (Dashboard, Whiteboards, My Library, etc.) were
   flickering and jumping to incorrect positions on every page reload. This was
   caused by a mismatch between the loading skeleton layout and the real sidebar,
   plus Material Symbols icon ligatures not being reserved space before the font
   loaded. Both root causes are fixed — the sidebar now renders in one smooth paint
   with zero cumulative layout shift (CLS).

🎨 Icon Rendering Polish
   Navigation icons now have fixed 22×22 px containers with proper flexbox
   centering and font-feature-settings for ligature glyph reservation, so they
   never flash or shift surrounding text during font load.

🔌 Extension v1.1.0
   - Manifest version bumped to 1.1.0
   - ESLint fixes in background.js and download.js
