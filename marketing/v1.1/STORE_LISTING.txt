Name
AntCapture: Record, Capture & Sync to Google Drive

Summary
Capture your screen, screenshots, and sync your media instantly to Google Drive — with real-time storage monitoring, a universal media library, and collaborative whiteboards.

Overview
AntCapture v1.1 brings a polished, professional dashboard experience with key reliability improvements. Now you can see your Google Drive storage quota in real time right inside the Command Center dashboard, alongside AntCapture cloud and whiteboard usage bars.

The sidebar navigation is now completely stable on page load — no more flickering or items jumping around when you reload the app. We also resolved a long-standing issue where the Google Drive progress bar was not appearing even for connected accounts.

Whether you are recording technical demos, documenting bugs, or saving visual references, AntCapture keeps your media organized across local storage and Google Drive with zero friction.

What's New in v1.1
• Google Drive quota bar now shows in the Command Center dashboard
• Sidebar navigation is stable on reload — zero layout shift
• Material Symbols icons no longer flash on first paint
• Extension ESLint cleanup and manifest updated to v1.1.0

Key Features
- Storage Control: Save captures locally, to Google Drive, or both simultaneously.
- Drive Quota Monitoring: See your Google Drive used/total storage right in your dashboard.
- Cloud Storage Bar: Track AntCapture cloud plan quota in real time.
- Universal Media Library: Browse your full capture history in a unified grid.
- Whiteboards: Create and manage unlimited collaborative whiteboards.
- Instant Previews: Preview image and video captures inside the library.
- Activity Tracking: View your weekly capture metrics.
- Built for Security: Captures stored only in your personal Google Drive — we never host your files.

How to Use
1. Click "Add to Chrome" and pin AntCapture to your toolbar.
2. Sign in securely with your Google account.
3. Click "Record Screen" or "Screenshot" from the popup.
4. Open the web dashboard to preview, manage, and monitor your storage.

Privacy & Architecture
AntCapture only accesses your screen when you explicitly trigger a capture. Your media is stored locally via IndexedDB or in your personal Google Drive — we do not host, view, or store your files on our servers. Authentication is handled through standard Google OAuth 2.0.
